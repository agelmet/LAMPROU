The doctor

Dr Lampros Lamprou, MD, PhD

Dr Lampros Lamprou, MD, PhD, is an ophthalmologist with a private practice in Melissia, Attica.

He graduated from the Medical School of the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens and holds a PhD from the Democritus University of Thrace.

He specialised in ophthalmology at the “Korgialenio – Benakio” Hellenic Red Cross Hospital in Athens.

He then moved to Sweden for further training in glaucoma, cataract and the macula. For many years he worked as a consultant at Skåne University Hospital (Skånes Universitetssjukhus) in Malmö/Lund and at Södra Älvsborg Hospital, where he was also head of glaucoma and responsible for training residents in the field.

Frequently asked questions

Didn’t find your answer? Ask the doctor.

Where is the practice?

In Melissia, north Athens, on the first floor of the shopping centre at 8 Leof. Dimokratias, right next to the Melissia Lidl. It is easy to reach from Vrilissia, Penteli and Nea Penteli.

Is there parking?

Yes, free. The shopping centre has its own free underground car park, with a lift that goes straight to the first floor, where the practice is.

Is the practice accessible for people with reduced mobility?

Yes. There is a dedicated parking space in the underground car park right next to the lift, and there is not a single step between your car and the door of the practice.

How do I book an appointment?

Online, through the practice’s Google appointment calendar, at any time that suits you. Or by phone on +30 211 424 7724 or +30 6977 627 626 during opening hours.

What are the opening hours?

Monday, Tuesday and Thursday 10:00–12:30 and 17:00–21:00, Friday 17:00–21:00 and Saturday 09:00–12:30. The practice is closed on Wednesday and Sunday.

Do you issue prescriptions for glasses?

Yes. The doctor issues electronic prescriptions for glasses, including for reimbursement through the Greek national health fund (EOPYY), after precise measurements with latest-generation equipment.

Do you see children?

Yes. The practice offers specialised care for both children and adults. Young children rarely complain that they cannot see well, so regular check-ups matter from infancy to adolescence.

Is everyone suitable for laser eye surgery?

No. Not every eye is suitable for refractive laser. A thorough pre-operative examination shows whether the procedure is completely safe for you and which technique suits each eye.

Does glaucoma have symptoms?

Usually not. High eye pressure rarely gives any warning sign or pain, which is why many people with glaucoma do not know they have it. Regular eye examinations are the only way to catch it early.
